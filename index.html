WHO WE ARE

A union built on purpose.

The Federation of Ifaki Students' Union (FISU) is a platform for unity, representation, advocacy and the advancement of students of Ifaki-Ekiti.

Through purposeful leadership, educational support, community engagement and student-centred initiatives, FISU works to create opportunities and strengthen the bond among Ifaki students.
